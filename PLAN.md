# MosquitoMo: 17-day pilot plan (2–18 October 2026)
NEMA deadline: Friday 23 October. Aim to submit by Wednesday 21 October to leave a buffer.

| Days | Dates | Do | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fri 2 Oct | App built (this package) | ✅ |
| 2 | Sat 3 Oct | Put it on GitHub Pages and set up Supabase (SETUP.md steps 1–2). Install on your own phone and one iPhone. | Live link opens and installs; a test report shows on dashboard.html |
| 3–4 | Sun 4–Mon 5 Oct | Test with 5 friends on different phones. Check readings for 6 places you know (Kampala, Iganga, Gulu, Kabale, Arua, Kasese) and note anything that looks wrong. | Bug list fixed; Kabale reads lower than Iganga |
| 4 | Mon 5 Oct | Follow-up with Prof. Bainomugisha: show the live app, ask about a supervisor and whether AirQo would mentor or host. Ask your Head of Department for the recommendation letter. | Supervisor named; letter requested |
| 5–6 | Tue 6–Wed 7 Oct | Pilot materials: QR-code poster, a WhatsApp message with the link, a 1-minute demo video. Choose 2 pilot sites, e.g. Makerere halls and Kawempe or Kalerwe (low ground near wetlands). | Materials printed and shared |
| 7 | Thu 8 Oct | Get the advice wording checked by a health worker, a VHT, or the District Health Office if you can. Optional: Luganda translation of `js/content.js` by a native speaker. | Wording reviewed |
| 8–14 | Fri 9–Thu 15 Oct | **Pilot week.** Share the link, run 2–3 demo sessions, check the dashboard every day, fix problems quickly. | 100+ devices, 40+ feedback forms, 20+ breeding-site reports |
| 15 | Fri 16 Oct | Sanity-check the index against what is known, e.g. the MoH weekly malaria bulletin or district seasonality. Write down where it agrees and where it doesn't. | One page of findings |
| 16 | Sat 17 Oct | Collect evidence: screenshots, dashboard figures, 5 user quotes, photos of demo sessions. | Evidence folder |
| 17 | Sun 18 Oct | Fill NEMA form sections 3–11 using the real pilot numbers. Build the budget for the 6-month pilot (under UGX 7M). | Draft form complete |
| — | 19–21 Oct | Signatures, declaration, CV, ID, testimonial, letters → one PDF. Submit online. | Submitted |

## Pilot indicators (they come straight from the dashboard for NEMA section 8)
- People using the app (devices) and readings checked
- Breeding sites reported, and how many local leaders or VHTs act on
- Understood reading, advice clarity and usefulness (1–5)
- Share of people who say they will take a protective action
- Recommend score (NPS)
