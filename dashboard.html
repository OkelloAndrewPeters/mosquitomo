What the numbers mean for the pilot

Understood, advice clarity and usefulness are averages on a 1–5 scale. “Will act” is the share of feedback where the person chose at least one protective action. Recommend score is the Net Promoter Score (−100 to 100). These are the indicators in the NEMA pilot results table.
